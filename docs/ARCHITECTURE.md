# Architecture

`lib/core/domain.dart` holds pure normalization, matching, candidate pool and Swiss-style pairing functions. `lib/data/bundled_name_repository.dart` copies the bundled read-only SQLite asset into the app sandbox once, then reads selected-country rankings without network access. `SessionStore` owns local app state; UI widgets only render state and dispatch actions. Its single SQLite state record is AES-256-GCM encrypted with a separate secure-storage key, and legacy preference state is migrated once then removed. Candidates are category-specific identities. The candidate combiner cycles country codes in lexical order, skips duplicate normalized category names, then seed-shuffles the completed pool. A No vote vetoes a match; Yes/Yes is Strong and every non-No mixed pair is Consider.

Face-off pairings are persisted before selection. A paired device records its own choices without scoring them; after the two encrypted round packets are exchanged, each applies the same scoring rule and advances deterministically. This keeps partner choices hidden until synchronization and makes a repeated packet idempotent.

The next-round scheduler orders by score, gives entries with fewer scored comparisons priority, avoids prior pairs where possible, and uses a session-seeded order for remaining ties. It persists each entry's left/right presentation count and chooses the orientation that minimizes side imbalance. An odd entry is left unpaired for that round (a non-scoring bye).

Face-off snapshots the shared, non-rejected shortlist plus local custom names (which may belong to either or both categories), persists pairings, scores, and progress, and runs up to seven Swiss-style rounds per enabled category. For pools over ten it may finish early only after every entry has at least three scored comparisons and the top-10 membership is unchanged in two consecutive completed rounds. Each partner records an independent ballot and encrypted cross-device round reconciliation applies identical scores. Rankings are deterministic: score, opponent strength, unanimous wins, initial seed tier, then session-seeded ordering. At the safety cap, a tie spanning the tenth-place boundary creates one persisted targeted tie-break round containing only that tied group.
